# Export saved search results

## Goal
Let analysts export a saved search without losing its filters.

## Plan
1. Define the export contract and preserve active filters.
2. Queue a background job that writes CSV and provides a download when ready.
3. Verify filters, quoting, empty results, retries, and expiration after 24 hours.

## Decisions
- Generate CSV asynchronously because large searches can time out a direct response.
- Keep completed downloads for 24 hours.
- Defer preview to keep the first release focused on reliable delivery.

None recorded.
